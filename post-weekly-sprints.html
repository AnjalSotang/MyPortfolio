A weekly sprint keeps my work balanced between shipping features and maintaining quality. I divide work into three buckets: deliverables, fixes, and growth tasks. This prevents overcommitting to only visible feature work.

Each Monday I pick one major milestone, two medium tasks, and a short buffer list. By Friday, I review what slipped and why. Over time, this feedback makes estimates far more realistic.

Simple Sprint Structure

  • One core outcome for the week.
  • Daily progress checkpoints under 10 minutes.
  • One reserved block for bug triage and refactor.
